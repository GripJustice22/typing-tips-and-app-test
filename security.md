## ⌨️ The Speed Revolution

Let's be honest. Watching someone type at 120 words per minute is mesmerizing. It looks like magic. But it's not magic; it is a learned physical skill, just like playing the piano or touch-typing. The difference between a 40 WPM typist and a 100 WPM typist is not intelligence; it is **muscle memory and technique**.

**Typing Tips And App Test** is the only repository you need to build that muscle memory. This is not just another typing test website. It is a complete training ecosystem. It combines over 50 scientifically-backed typing tips, a customizable test application, and a detailed analytics dashboard that tracks your progress over time.

Whether you are a student writing essays, a programmer typing code, or a professional answering emails, increasing your typing speed will save you hours of time every week. This repository gives you the exact drills, the correct finger placements, and the motivation to finally break through your typing plateau.

## 🏗️ How The Training Works

We have organized the repository into three distinct training modules.

### Module 1: The Tip Library
Before you start typing, you must understand the *theory*. Our `tips/` folder contains 50 detailed Markdown files. Each tip covers a specific aspect of typing: from the correct posture and wrist placement to advanced techniques like "chorded typing" and "rolling." Every tip includes a visual diagram and a practice exercise.

### Module 2: The Test App
The core of the repository is the `app/` folder. This is a fully functional, web-based typing test. It features:
- **Multiple Modes:** Test yourself with random words, quotes, or code snippets.
- **Live Feedback:** The text changes color as you type. Correct letters are green, errors are red.
- **Heatmap:** After the test, the app generates a heatmap of your keyboard, showing which keys you struggle with the most.
- **Customizable Timers:** Test yourself for 15, 30, 60, or 120 seconds.

### Module 3: The Progress Tracker
The app stores your results in your browser's LocalStorage. You can view a beautiful line chart showing your WPM and accuracy over the last 30 days. It also calculates your average speed and highlights your longest streak of error-free typing.

## 🧠 Under The Hood: The Science of Typing

Why do some people type faster than others? The answer lies in **kinesthetic memory**. When you first learn to type, you use your visual cortex to find each key. This is slow and exhausting. Over time, your brain builds a mental map of the keyboard, and your fingers begin to move automatically.

Our test app is designed to accelerate this process. It uses a technique called **"Deliberate Practice."** Instead of just typing random words, you can select specific drills that target your weakest keys. If the heatmap shows you struggle with the 'P' and 'Q' keys, you can generate a drill that heavily features those letters.

The app is built using Vanilla JavaScript. It doesn't use any heavy frameworks like React or Vue. This means the app loads instantly and responds to your keystrokes with zero lag. This is crucial because even a 50ms delay in visual feedback can throw off a fast typist.
